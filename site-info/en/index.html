What does the app do?

  • Wind compass: where the wind comes from and which way to turn the cap
  • Wind force in Beaufort and m/s, gusts shown separately, and a milling-weather verdict
  • Warning when the wind shifts more than 45° in the next 12 hours
  • Local notifications for wind shifts and strong wind — no server, no account
  • Discover: every existing windmill on the map, with photo, type, year built and opening hours
  • Free and open source (MIT), in 5 languages; tips are optional
