Who is responsible?

The IT Crowd (Ed Cafferata), based in the Netherlands, builds and runs this app.

What data?

Molenwind collects no personal data: no account, no sign-in, no analytics or ad trackers. If you allow it, the app uses your location only on your device to show nearby windmills; your location is never sent anywhere. Your chosen mill and settings (such as notifications on/off) are stored only locally on your device.

Where is it stored?

We run no server. For the wind forecast the app only sends the coordinates of the chosen mill (not your location) to Open-Meteo. Mill photos load from the image bank of the Dutch Cultural Heritage Agency (RCE), maps via Apple Maps. Like any website, these services see your IP address. Tips go through Apple (In-App Purchase); we receive no payment or personal data.

Your rights (GDPR)

Because we keep no accounts or personal data on our servers, we normally hold nothing about you. Still have a question about your data or your GDPR rights (access, rectification, erasure, objection)? Email us.
