FAQ

  • Where does the wind data come from? From the KNMI Harmonie model (2 km) via Open-Meteo, for the exact location of your mill.
  • Do I need an account? No. Molenwind works without an account or sign-in.
  • Why don't I get notifications? Turn notifications on in the app and in Settings → Notifications → Molenwind. iOS refreshes the forecast now and then in the background; Background App Refresh must be on.
  • Opening hours are missing or a mill is wrong. Opening hours come from OpenStreetMap (opening_hours) and mill data from the RCE. Email us, or add it yourself in OpenStreetMap.
  • What does it cost? Nothing. All features are free; in-app tips are optional and support development.
  • Can I contribute? Yes, the source code is at github.com/EdCafferata/molenwind.
