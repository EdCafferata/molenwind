Molenwind needs no account or login. All features are available right away. Tip: on the "Mijn molen" tab, pick a windmill (for example "Op Hoop van Beter", Ingen) to see the live wind compass and forecast. Location permission is optional and only used on-device to list nearby windmills. The four in-app purchases on the "Over" tab are optional consumable tips; they unlock nothing. Weather data comes from Open-Meteo (no key), windmill data from the Dutch Cultural Heritage Agency (public domain). The app is open source: github.com/EdCafferata/molenwind
