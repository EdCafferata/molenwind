Molenwind is a free app for windmill millers and windmill lovers in the Netherlands.

MY MILL, FOR THE MILLER
• Pick your own windmill; with your location, nearby mills are listed first.
• Wind compass: where the wind comes from and which way to turn the cap.
• Wind force in Beaufort and m/s, gusts shown separately, and a milling verdict: too little wind, good milling weather, strong or stop.
• Warning when the wind is set to shift by more than 45° in the next 12 hours.
• Hourly forecast and a satellite map zoomed in on your mill, with a wind arrow.
• Notifications for a wind shift (30 minutes ahead) and for strong wind (1 hour ahead).

DISCOVER, FOR EVERYONE
• More than 1,200 Dutch windmills on the map, grouped by region.
• Mill page with photo, type, purpose, year built and whether it is a good day for turning.
• Opening hours with "open now" where known, plus directions and a link to allemolens.nl.

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your location stays on your phone.

OPEN SOURCE
The source code is on GitHub (EdCafferata/molenwind). All features are free. If you like, you can leave an optional tip in the app.

Sources: windmill data from the Dutch Cultural Heritage Agency (RCE), opening hours from OpenStreetMap, weather data from Open-Meteo.
